## Question

Does the exact composed candidate `8f939ada…0b0c62` retain V4 outcomes across
all 30 saved loss fixtures and all 20 saved top-team fixtures, while adding the
Civitasmass Pizza/Ice Cream rescue?

This panel combines 60 loss seat-games and 40 top20 seat-games. It runs the
composed candidate once on each frozen replay and compares each row with the
recorded direct V4 result. The two Pizza target seats are allowed to differ;
the remaining 98 rows must match V4 exactly in result, rewards, margin, status,
and frame count. The four Ghost/Kwa target seats remain active and must still
win with unchanged V4 outcomes.

- The other 98 rows match direct V4 results, rewards, margins, statuses, and frames.

This is saved-tape evidence only. It does not establish performance against
reacting opponents or promotion readiness. `main.py` remains unchanged; Kaggle
is out of scope.
