# Lower-cost production feasibility audit

Frozen 2026-09-27 before outputs. This is a passive feasibility audit, not
a candidate, strength test or authorization to alter main.py.

The rejected auxiliary field sold48 extra tomatoes but paid a fourth-land
purchase and two additional Fibonacci wages for12 days. Test whether its
physical commitment can be made materially cheaper, before another game.

Use all nine exact baseline traces from the two existing live cash audits.
Keep their hashes and episode IDs. Inspect starts on days13–18, with a
12-day horizon. Report current empty/weed tiles in owned land, tiles left
empty throughout the factual horizon, minimum and maximum daily hired
hands, and full marginal wages for one versus two new workers. Inspect
the baseline farmer/hands for terminal daily idle windows of at least
four actions, including position and inventory. Do not count missing
workers as available labor. A window is idle only if all remaining
commands are PASS; otherwise baseline tasks need an explicit new schedule.

These recorded-state capacities cannot become runtime conditions. They
are diagnostic constraints for a later observable-state controller.
Unused cells do not prove work feasibility, and spare actions do not prove
a complete crop schedule. A new candidate needs a coherent planting,
watering, physical supply, harvest and delivery schedule first.

Accept a lower-cost mechanism for further mechanical design only if a
specific six-cell owned-land block can remain free of baseline crop
commitments for12 days in at least one demand-eligible case, or baseline
worker windows can explicitly support all required daily work. Otherwise
reject this route to saving land/wages. No claim of native strength and
no promotion based on this audit.
