# Compatible complete routes for the remaining top-20 losses

Frozen 2026-09-28 before candidate outcomes. Offline work only.

## Why this is a separate experiment

The opening-fork v2 screen leaves all three current top-20 failures lost.
A previous feasibility audit found three existing, fully funded schedules
with identical actions through step 143. It rejected them as a broad fix
because their activation covers only one of the 30 public losses and five
top-20 fixtures. Here their narrower purpose is explicit: a complementary
top-20 repair judged by wins, with separate reacting qualification required.
The earlier route-portfolio outcome screen used different mappings and an
older main file; its margin threshold is not evidence about these variants.

First play DECEM, Boey and Vadim in both seats, together with the two
affected winning controls Yizhou and Kaggledew Valley, on their exact
saved seeds and replies from manifest SHA
`524bc1bfa2f852b3865b94c4d7a37c67be9cc56690b56d2ed0a9a201e4412e20`.
Native 1.32.7, unmodified shop generation, 720 turns; candidate seed hidden.
The pilot passes only if all ten games complete without policy errors,
at least one of the three failed fixtures becomes a both-seat win, and
both previously won controls remain both-seat wins. A failure rejects
this exact candidate; do not fit a trigger after seeing these outcomes.

If it passes, run all 50 saved fixtures in both seats and require no
formerly won seat to become a draw/loss plus at least one new both-seat
win. These remain development data. Before any native reacting outcomes,
freeze the exact candidate hash, fresh activation-scan seed ranges,
reacting references and paired win-point gates in a separate native plan.
Sparse activation must be measured without filtering by final reward.

No promotion based on tapes, no main edit and no Kaggle upload. The full
user objective remains 30/30 public-loss replies and 20/20 top-team replies
won in both seats; this bounded experiment does not replace that objective.
