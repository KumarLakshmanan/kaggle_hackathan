Both new losses have **zero failed own purchases**. No evidence supports
a missing-purchase repair for these two episodes. The winning controls also
have zero failed own purchases. The existing funding candidate is being
evaluated under its separate frozen plan, not credited with these fixes.

## Decision

**Accept the passive diagnosis; reject a claim that purchase failures
explain these new losses.** No policy change, promotion, or upload. Investigate
a complete early crop schedule with funding, worker travel, later crop
commitments and rival market response if proposing a new intervention.
The melon timing comparison is descriptive, not proof that transplanting
the rival schedule will win fresh reacting games.
