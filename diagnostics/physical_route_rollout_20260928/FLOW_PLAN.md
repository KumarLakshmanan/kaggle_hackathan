# Observable rival net-trade inference — 2026-09-28

Separate engineering check, frozen after native_core physical parity and
before inference results. No competition candidate or outcome experiment.

At a non-midnight turn, reconstruct our own post-worker shed exactly from
our prior legal observation and the action we issued. Our observed next
shed minus that reconstruction gives our net market purchase. Shared market
inventory change plus known town consumption plus our net purchase then
equals the rival's net sale. This uses our private stock, public farm/market/
town state and our own prior action; it uses neither rival private inventory
nor the rival's action. Skip midnight: automatic deposits and overflow make
the same inversion ambiguous.

Validate both seats on all non-midnight transitions of the same four frozen
source replays. The harness may use rival private state/actions only to form
an independent accounting truth from actual rival shed changes. Require
exact equality for every product on all 5,520 eligible seat-transitions.
Record coverage and signed-flow activity. No threshold fitting, future
prediction, candidate decisions or alternative route outcomes are included.

If successful, this establishes an observable history feature, not knowledge
of gross rival queues or future supply. Net flow cannot identify same-turn
buy/sell round trips. A future forecast must retain that uncertainty and
earn its own prediction and reacting-policy checks.
