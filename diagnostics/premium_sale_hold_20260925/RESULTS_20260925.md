# Premium sale-hold experiment — 2026-09-25

## Finding

No supportable material terminal-cash gain emerged for the two selected losses. In ActiveMusyoku e112941285—the low-strawberry-demand target—the wrapper withheld strawberry sell orders, but strawberry units sold were unchanged, strawberry receipts were essentially unchanged and slightly lower, and own terminal cash fell by $889. Excluding e112933589 also fell ($147). The two winning controls gained $888 and $1,293, but those gains came from milk/wool-side holds rather than strawberry holds. This four-route, fixed-tape result is mixed and is not evidence for deploying the policy in the live audit.

## Design and verification

- Exact installed public Kaggriculture engine: `1.32.7`.
- Frozen baseline `main.py` SHA-256: `04b0bdc3bbe319d969170ddc8007bd6300af254a98151d6ef990c9ed2ebbabc1`; the replay harness refused to run if this changed. `main.py` was not edited by this experiment.
- Cohort was selected before treatment from the existing 12-route ledger: losses ActiveMusyoku e112941285 and Excluding e112933589, paired with winning controls Gleb Tumanov e112939403 and HowardLeeTW e112935831.
- The exact 719-action route tapes were digest-checked against the existing manifest. Each route was run in both seats, against the same raw opponent tape.
- Baseline: 8/8 games completed. Treatment: 8/8 fixed-shop and 8/8 native games completed. Every run returned `DONE` for both players; each candidate made 719 calls. Fixed-shop and native terminal cash/stock outcomes matched route-for-route and seat-for-seat in this cohort.
- Fixed-shop forced each candidate seat's baseline shop sequence; native used normal shop draws. These are paired replays of four frozen routes, not independent-seed validation.
- Wrapper inputs are current public observations plus the baseline agent's own proposed action. It only reduces current SELL quantities, with a capacity buffer; it reads no route identity, seed, future shop draw, or private opponent state.

The public rules make this hypothesis plausible but constrained: market prices are inventory-sensitive, the shed is capped at 100, and end-of-day carried overflow is discarded. See the [official rules](https://github.com/Kaggle/kaggle-environments/blob/master/kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/README.md) and [engine source](https://github.com/Kaggle/kaggle-environments/blob/master/kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py).

## Baseline opportunity

On ActiveMusyoku, the visible eight-shop sequence was `PIZZA_SHOP, BAKERY, BAKERY, PET_CAFE, PIZZA_SHOP, PET_CAFE, SMOOTHIE_SHOP, YARN_STORE`; a strawberry-consuming shop appears only late. Baseline strawberry orders covered 282 units; 247 sold for $2,648 (mean $10.72). Of those sales, 98 occurred when no currently unlocked strawberry consumer was visible, only 14 fell on scheduled strawberry-demand pulses, and the one-step observable quote-up cash proxy was $0. The baseline trace's strawberry quote range was $1–$151. There was physical capacity headroom (up to 72 units by the recorded safe-hold measure), but no own overflow and no unsold terminal stock to recover.

For comparison, Excluding sold 249/291 strawberry units for $25,921, with a $16 one-step quote-up proxy and 75-unit maximum safe-hold headroom. HowardLeeTW and Gleb Tumanov sold 249/270 for $45,997 and 247/287 for $23,655 respectively. Thus the controls did not supply evidence that the Active strawberry glut had a profitable observable release window.

On Active, the wrapper recorded 352 cumulative strawberry sell-order units withheld across decisions (not a count of unique physical units). It did not raise strawberry units sold; receipts decreased $20. Its wider premium policy also held milk/wool, and the route's net own-cash loss was $889. Strawberry was not held on either winning control; their own-cash gains therefore do not support the strawberry hypothesis.

Sheds still reached 99–100/100 in the traces. The wrapper did not systematically reduce overflow: Active remained at zero, Excluding remained at four units, and only HowardLeeTW improved from two to zero. All four baselines and all treatments ended with zero own shed/carried stock.

## Latency

All runs used 719 candidate calls. Baseline cumulative agent time ranged 1.19–1.82 s per game (1.65–2.54 ms/call); wrapper time ranged 2.14–2.78 s fixed-shop and 1.95–2.96 s native (2.72–4.12 ms/call). The slowest single wrapper call was 0.445 s fixed-shop and 0.281 s native. No run timed out.

## Disposition and artifacts

This is a negative result for the targeted low-demand strawberry opportunity and an inconclusive mixed result for the broader premium basket. Do not promote this rule to `main.py` or the live audit on this evidence. The standalone wrapper remains only as the requested reproducible experiment artifact.
