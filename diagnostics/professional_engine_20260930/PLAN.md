## Model separation and execution budget

Saved episode group ID modulo5 is a fixed train/holdout split. No episode may
appear in both groups, and no hidden rival private fields are input features.
The fitted model reports uncertainty; it is not a guarantee of a future score.
Public replay samples from the saved panel make that panel development evidence.
The fresh native seed block remains independent of model fitting.

Online queue search and macro rollouts use fixed transition/node budgets.
Only fully completed, paired forecast horizons can activate a macro. Partial
search never becomes a forecast of final-season coins. Native/runtime checks
decide operational acceptance; increasing search depth alone earns no promotion.
