inventory does not change that screen's candidates, ranking, gates or pool.
It supports a later separately declared selector study.

public wins, both source-policy seats where matched prefixes are simulated.

current market and revealed shops, and our own private state. Extract crop
and animal counts, hand count, public cash and owned quadrants. No future
shops, rival private holdings, terminal rewards or replay IDs become policy
features. Fixture/seed/hash fields in this research ledger are provenance.

- Recorded-public inventory reads each original replay at72. For top-team
  fixtures its opposite player is another recorded peer, not our367 agent.
  This inventory is not a matched candidate decision state.
- Matched inventory runs exact367 from the verified original initial state
  against each saved opponent action tape for exactly72 transitions, in
  each seat. Hide configuration seed from the policy. Preserve its complete
  action hash and observation state, statuses and telemetry. Stop before
  calling the step72 decision. These are descriptive prefixes, not new
  strength games. One worker, sequentially after the live target screen.

Bind inputs and helpers, checkpoint uniquely by fixture/seat, verify all
recorded raw/tape hashes, and compare matching existing residual traces
where available as a consistency check. Keep recorded and matched fields
explicitly labeled; never substitute one for the other.
