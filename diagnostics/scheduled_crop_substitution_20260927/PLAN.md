# Existing-schedule crop substitution feasibility

Frozen 2026-09-27 before outputs. Passive mechanical diagnosis only.

The prior crop-conversion audit concerned melon and found insufficient
replacement work. The separate extra tomato field worked physically but
lost wins after capital/wages and market changes. Here inspect replacing
a planned late strawberry planting with tomato while retaining every
existing worker position and crop command. This adds no land or worker.

Use all nine hash-verified incumbent live traces already cash-audited.
Inspect every successful strawberry planting from zero-based days12–17.
Replay the native single-tile mechanics from its planting through its next
DIG/new planting or the game end. First require exact strawberry tile-state
parity on each turn, including fertilization, water, harvest and decay.
Then substitute tomato in that isolated tile, using the same observed
WATER/FERTILIZE/HARVEST commands and actual physical fertilizer availability.
Report both crops' harvest counts and times, fertilizer use, and the tomato
death/decay time. Do not invent an earlier harvest or more fertilizer.

Report observed shops at planting. Quote-valued harvests using recorded
prices are explicitly hindsight diagnostics, not deployable forecasts,
realized cash or a promotion criterion. Delivery/market changes and shared
price response remain untested. A candidate would need a prospective
observable-state selection rule and a coherent seed/delivery/sale plan.

Accept this mechanism for further design only if at least one demand-
eligible plot (at least two visible tomato shops) harvests at least four
tomato units under the unchanged schedule, with verified baseline parity.
Otherwise reject before building a candidate. No main edit or upload.
