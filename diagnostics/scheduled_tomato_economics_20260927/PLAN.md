Frozen 2026-09-27 before outputs. No deployable candidate or strength test.
Use all28 demand-eligible plots from the accepted mechanical audit:14 in
Driz Lo and14 in the first liminhai game. Both source traces are exact
incumbent native reproductions. Include the winning control and losing case.

Reconstruct every recorded market unit with the installed native engine,
including simultaneous quotes, and verify each turn's original cash.
Then price an idealized physical substitution along the same recorded shop
and trade paths. All other players' quantities/actions remain factual.
This is a causal diagnostic with hindsight data, never policy validation.

Each selected planting replaces an8-unit strawberry commitment with the
mechanically demonstrated4-unit tomato schedule. Estimate delivery at the
worker's next legal product DROP/PLACE or the midnight automatic drop.
Track the removed strawberry inventory until it prevents an original sale.

the next turn if all10 order slots are already used. Record any unmodeled
capacity or delivery assumptions. Credit the50-coin seed cost difference
at planting; no extra worker or land cost. Ignore saved fertilizer receipts
and their market effects, and report this omission explicitly.

Propagate the changed market inventory into every later unit quote for both
players, retaining native simultaneous quoting within each unit pair. Report
own/rival cash changes, paired margin change, canceled strawberry sales,
added tomato sales, residual removed stock, assumed delivery quantities,
and any negative projected cash. This is neither a realized win nor an
upper/lower bound on the reactive policy's value.

Accept further candidate design only if the losing liminhai case improves
in relative cash, the Driz winning control remains positive, and all
modeled cash stays nonnegative. Otherwise reject this specific substitution
direction before building it. Passing does not qualify promotion: complete
delivery/seed accounting and original-shop reactive tests are still required.
