opponent actions. Its original market unlocks repeatedly include Bakeries and
Pet Cafes and no Yarn Store. Our early position is ahead, but the opponent
overtakes around day 20. Both final shed stocks are empty, so the issue is
production allocation, sale prices, and timing rather than an unsold endgame
pile. The trace ledger reconciles every dollar of final cash.

## Crop-cohort experiments

An experimental day-11 substitution of a 13-tile strawberry cohort exposed a
critical execution dependency: a newly replanted carrot must be watered on
its planting day. A first version reused later inherited WATER actions for
PLANT without coordinating another same-day WATER; 12 plots became weeds.
It is invalid as a crop-rotation mechanism and was rejected.

Single-cycle substitutions under the new loss's original shops were:

| Cohort action | Seat-0 margin | Native seat-0 margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Unchanged main | −6,073 | −6,073 |
| Omit the 13 late strawberries | −4,678 | not tested |
| Carrots, one cycle | −4,608 | −8,251 |
| Wheat, one cycle | −3,761 | −7,745 |
| Route 110 plus wheat, one cycle | −1,090 | −5,577 |

The wheat rule did **not** generalize. On the older 40-route panel it fell from
37/40 to 34/40 route wins and from 74/80 to 69/80 seat wins, reversing three
previous winning routes. On a new rank-10 sampled route it reversed a +11,780
per-seat win into a −5,108 loss. None of these crop rules was promoted.
This is why no future-shop-path win or isolated fixed-shop cash gain is enough
to justify changing the submission agent.

## Decision gate

The current main is unchanged while we test a small, fully scheduled crop
block: seed purchase **before** planting, same-day watering, harvest and
delivery, priced at the expected sale date, with explicit worker opportunity
cost. That experiment may be promoted only if it improves a known loss under
its original shops without reversing existing wins on the refreshed first-20
panel or wider regression sets. No claim of beating all possible agents can be
established from these captured episodes.
