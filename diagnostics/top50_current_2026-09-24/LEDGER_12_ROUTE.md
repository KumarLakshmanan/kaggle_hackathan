## Study boundary and audit

This completes the **six predeclared losses plus six matched winning controls, both candidate seats: 24 native games** in engine 1.32.7. The panel and its matching rule were frozen in [loss_clusters.md](loss_clusters.md) before these games; that document's “not yet run” section is historical and is superseded here. The panel is drawn from the 50-team/100-route/33-loss fixed-tape benchmark, not a fresh leaderboard sample. The six losses are both routes of rank-25 Excluding, rank-35 Boey, and rank-49 ActiveMusyoku. Controls are distinct-team, both-seat-winning routes in the same rank decile, chosen by public source cash/rank/seat; they are **not** exchangeable counterfactuals.

The [sidecar](loss_cluster_sidecar_20260924.py) rechecked the source manifest, all tape action hashes and lengths, benchmark outcomes, and frozen `main.py` SHA-256 `04b0bdc3bbe319d969170ddc8007bd6300af254a98151d6ef990c9ed2ebbabc1`. Every run finished `DONE` and exactly reproduced the benchmark's candidate cash, rival cash, and margin. The bounded [compact artifact](ledger_12_route_compact_20260924.json.gz) (132,628 bytes; SHA-256 `2e05158830c82bad3390ea3c30e93ce9ec4ff954dc8ca1461d9474df3b53b875`) holds 30 days per game, not raw turns or hidden opponent state. **All 720 own daily ledgers reconcile:** day opening cash + successful own `SELL` receipts − successful own market cash costs = day closing cash; day-0 opening is 3,000 and day-29 closing equals final reward. For example, Excluding 112933084/seat 0/day 18 is `43,550 + 5,704 − 5,220 = 44,034`. Costs include executed seed/animal/product purchases, hire, land, and other negative market commits; attempted orders are not treated as cash flows.

Each daily row also stores own executed sale units/receipts by product, confirmed harvest units and farm-action counts, public shops/prices, and observed **public** own/rival farm cohorts and rival cash. Farm and rival-cash snapshots are taken at the **start** of the zero-based day; own `close` is after that day's actions. Final rival cash comes from the game outcome, not the start-of-day-29 snapshot. We did not reconstruct rival sales, inventory, costs, or product profit from its source tape.

## Mechanism classification from executed economics

1. **Realized price/market path, with comparable own service and units.** Excluding 112933589 and its control each planted 33 strawberry tiles, confirmed 303 strawberry water actions, harvested 249, and sold 249. Yet own strawberry receipts were **20,076 lower**, at 104.10 versus 184.73 per executed unit. ActiveMusyoku 112941285 and its control likewise each planted 33, confirmed 303 waters, harvested/sold 247, but receipts were **21,007 lower**, at 10.72 versus 95.77 per unit. Its day-15 own cash was still ahead (28,252 vs 24,672), but by day 18 was behind (34,301 vs 37,662); final public rival cash was actually 838 *lower* than the control's. These are strong **own-cash realization** examples, not missing strawberry cohorts. They do **not** identify shops alone as the price cause: sale timing, public market stock, and both farms' executed supply differ. A prior, separate modified-environment test on ActiveMusyoku 112941285 kept the first two shops fixed, forced later unlocks, and moved own cash 60,243→118,160 while strawberry units stayed 247; that is shop-path sensitivity, **not one of these 24 native games** or a single-price causal estimate.

2. **Capacity/mix/timing plus price, not an isolated service shortfall.** Boey 112939032 had 5 publicly visible sheep versus 15 for its control; confirmed placements were 5/15, feed actions 85/283, care 120/289, wool harvested 91/329 and sold 91/327. Own wool receipts were 67,861 lower. A symmetric quantity/average-price accounting split attributes about **33,644** of that receipt gap to fewer sold units and **34,217** to lower realized price (60.70 vs 224.42); this is an identity, not a causal decomposition. Boey 112940236 similarly had 6 vs 9 cows, 162 vs 239 milk units sold/harvested, and 10,649 less milk cash; about 5,570 is quantity and 5,079 price by the same arithmetic. ActiveMusyoku 112937075 instead allocated toward cows (9 vs 6) and away from sheep (5 vs 11): milk receipts were **24,093 higher**, wool **24,038 lower**, and strawberry **12,673 lower** despite 249 harvests in both routes. Absolute feed/care differences largely accompany different herd sizes, and crop/animal ages and shop paths differ. The ledger finds **no clean comparable-capacity cohort-service failure**; it does not prove service is optimal.

3. **Public rival cash can dominate an otherwise healthy own ledger.** In Excluding 112933084, our own receipts exceeded the winning control by 7,837 but our costs exceeded it by 8,662, leaving only **825 less own cash**. The rival finished **41,526 above** the control rival. Within the loss game the rival's public tomato area rose from 10 tiles at day 15 to 34 at day 18, while ours remained at zero until ten plants were placed on day 18 (visible on day 19); our 80 executed tomato sales ultimately brought 16,362. Rival **public cash at the start** of day 24 was 61,236 and at the start of day 27 was 127,701, ending at 155,219. This supports a late *competitive-capacity/timing hypothesis*, not a claim about rival tomato receipts or hidden inventory, nor proof that buying extra own tomato land would pay for displaced work. This route is chiefly **opponent-cash-led** in the paired descriptive contrast; ActiveMusyoku 112941285 is chiefly **own-realization-led**. The remaining four combine own shortfall and rival strength.

## Controlled next experiments and limits

- **Price guard:** On public shops, current public market quotes/stock, own tiles, remaining days, labor, and shed capacity, test a bounded rule that stops *new* low-value production commitments; do not use team, seed, or episode IDs. A/B on the **same route/seat**, first forcing the baseline shop sequence in both arms and then using native shop RNG. Track confirmed commitments, service, harvested/sold units, receipts, costs, own cash, and rival final cash separately. Check adverse and favorable demand paths; don't promote from these two strawberry cases.
- **Incremental capacity or mix:** Predeclare 0/2/4 feasible tomato, sheep, or cow lanes one product at a time, with cash/land/worker/harvest/sale-capacity certificates and opportunity cost of displaced work. Evaluate under matched forced shops, then native shops and unseen routes. Excluding's existing ten-tomato branch means the tomato test is *earlier or incremental* capacity, not “add tomatoes.” A past four-tile pilot did not generalize.
- **Service residual:** Only after matching public cohort count/age and shop path, hold commitments fixed and vary one observation-legal water/feed/care/harvest schedule. Require higher **executed** yield and net own cash, not merely more requested actions. The present six contrasts lack that isolation; no broad service allocator is justified.

All games still use **static opponent action tapes**. Recorded source rewards are from different original matchups: Excluding 112933084's tape earned 115,320 originally but 155,219 in this replay (+39,899); the other five loss tapes differ from source by only −140 to +949. Replay effects can therefore alter rival cash sharply without the rival adapting. Shop RNG may change when a policy changes tile occupancy, even on the same seed; forcing shops controls that path but modifies the environment and still leaves market/opponent responses. The rank-25/35/49, two-route-per-team panel was selected for diagnosis, not prevalence estimation across all 33 losses: even within rank-25 Excluding, rival replay cash changes from 155,219 to 102,267 between episodes, and the two rank-49 ActiveMusyoku routes have own cash 79,685 versus 60,243. Rank or team identity is not a policy signal. Missing evidence for a policy decision is a same-world single-mechanism A/B, comparable-cohort service residuals, unseen route validation, and a reactive-opponent check. No opponent private inventory or gross tape `SELL` intent is treated as an executed cash ledger. In the compact schema, `PLACE:<product>` can denote a confirmed product deposit, not a new production tile; plant/animal capacity claims use public tiles and confirmed `PLANT`/animal `PLACE` instead.
