[consultation](https://chatgpt.com/c/6ab4f501-73e0-83ee-92a4-6283e49dd350)

The most useful correction was to avoid explaining all 33/100 route losses as
one market-timing bug. It ranked (1) early commitment to complete production
cohorts, (2) whole-farm task/service feasibility, and (3) realized inventory
value. First, trace exact own cash, funded commitments, servicing, quantity,
market prices and first consequential divergence on a small predeclared panel:
both losing episodes from three double-loss teams and six matched winning
controls, both candidate seats. Do not infer private opponent inventory from
its fixed action tape.

If that diagnosis confirms an investment gap, pilot a next-cohort allocator
*before* irreversible purchases. Compare the incumbent, deferral, small single-
animal cohorts and a mixed cohort. Value a complete cohort through terminal
cash, including construction, feed, labor, transport, market-order capacity and
effects on existing output, under low/base/high observable supply scenarios.
Use one authoritative reservation ledger; abstain before commitment on a failed
certificate and finish or safely unwind any already-committed cohort. A single
four-tile perturbation is not a substitute for a production scheduler.

Suggested engineering gate (not a statistical guarantee): diagnose on the
preselected losses/controls first; then require a full native 100-route result
of at least 72 route wins and 143/200 seat wins versus frozen 67 and 133,
at most two route reversals and four seat reversals, plus original-shop-path
fixed-environment gains relative to its **separately measured** baseline.
Also check the bottom-ten route margins, all games DONE, per-turn runtime,
and fresh reactive public agents. Native, fixed-shop and reactive tests answer
different questions and should never be merged into one headline score.

## Follow-up after the 24-game ledger and early-tomato pilot

A second aggregate-only brief reported equal-unit low-price strawberry losses,
the absence of a comparable-cohort service failure, the two-plot pilot's own-
cash regression despite positive paired margin, and the forced-route control
reversals. GPT-6 Pro [revised its priority](https://chatgpt.com/c/6ab4f501-73e0-83ee-92a4-6283e49dd350): calibrate a short-horizon **own-cash market-realization** counterfactual before more production swaps. This does not mean the 20k–21k cross-route receipt gaps are recoverable; the routes had different shop paths.

Its smallest proposed test defers at most four already-shed strawberry units
from one existing `SELL` order for **exactly one turn**, across known active
consumption but no day refresh/shop unlock. It keeps at least one unit in the
original order, reserves stock and a next-turn order slot, protects funded
obligations, and settles regardless of whether the next quote is favorable.
First validate exact transaction mechanics, then forecast the *paired change*
in own cash at settlement rather than a baseline price. Compare that forecast
with actual coupled branches, track optimism and a frozen error allowance,
and measure own **terminal** cash separately from immediate receipts and rival
cash. Baseline and candidate must be run under the same fixed shop schedule;
native shop dynamics remain a distinct test.

Stop if the sale model does not reconcile, the forecast edge is no larger than
its calibration error, exposure is inadequate, own cash falls, or a previously
winning native control reverses. A positive paired margin caused only by lower
fixed-tape rival cash is not an own-profit success. GPT-6 Pro proposed 68/100
native route wins and 134/200 seat wins with no prior winning outcome reversed
as a *minimum* promotion threshold for this small mechanism; it is not a
predicted result. A subsequent installed-engine scope check bounded the
direct q≤4, one-turn strawberry-demand uplift to about 69 coins before other
market-order interactions. That is far too small for the severe-loss question,
so this specific sale-delay policy was not implemented or simulated. It has no
measured terminal effect.

Its proposed offline menu is Full, Half (ceiling of N/2), Omit, and Stage
(half now, reconsider the rest once after the next observed shop reveal).
Select the earliest eligible unpurchased project after public demand exists;
apply the same checkpoint rule to losses and controls. A valid branch must
update purchase, work, inventory and future-service reservations together,
and compare **own terminal cash**, not gross crop revenue or paired margin
alone. Unknown future shops should be sampled from a frozen public-rule
distribution shared across alternatives, not read from a replay; competing
supply and adverse demand remain uncertainty cases. Forecast differences

The proposed stop gate is that reduced/staged commitments must yield at least

least two economic families, with an observable pre-commitment discriminator.

promotion, the proposed engineering gate is at least 70/100 paired-route and
139/200 seat wins, no previously winning route/seat reversed, positive own
cash under both native and separately recomputed fixed-shop controls, and at
least two fixed-shop loss rescues attributable to own cash. These are test
thresholds, **not** a forecast of score or rank. No such sizing branch has
